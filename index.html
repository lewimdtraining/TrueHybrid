Nutrition Tool.

Set your calories and macros from your own numbers, tell it the foods you like and the ones you don't, then build a week of meals from 200 recipes. Bulk-prep the same meals across the week, or eat for variety. Everything runs in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.

1. Your numbers

Used to estimate your daily energy and macro targets. Rough is fine, you can override the target below.
On the deficit and surplus: these are kept in a sustainable range on purpose. Cutting much harder than this under-fuels your training and recovery; a bigger surplus is mostly fat, not muscle. A calorie floor also stops the target dropping too low. Slower and steady wins here.
Read this first. This tool gives a general estimate, not medical or dietetic advice. The figures come from a standard equation and a food database, so they are a starting point to adjust from, not a prescription. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, managing a medical condition such as diabetes or kidney disease, taking medication that interacts with diet, or have a history of disordered eating, talk to a doctor or an accredited dietitian before changing how you eat. Stop and seek advice if you feel unwell.

2. Your daily target

Protein set from bodyweight, fats at 25% of calories, carbs fill the rest. Edit any value and the plan will aim at it.

Your week

Delivered per day, averaged across the week. Orange means more than 10% over target on calories, carbs, or fats.
